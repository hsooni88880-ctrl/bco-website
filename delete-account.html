Delete Your NOVA Account

Use this page to request deletion of your NOVA account and associated personal data.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What will be deleted?

When your request is verified and processed, NOVA will delete eligible account and associated personal data, including account/profile information and user-generated data associated with the account where applicable.

Some information may be retained for a limited period when required by law or necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal or technical reasons.

How to request deletion

Send an email to bco.app.team@gmail.com with the email address associated with your NOVA account.

Verification and processing

For security, we may request reasonable information needed to verify account ownership. After verification, we will process the request in accordance with our applicable policies and legal requirements.
