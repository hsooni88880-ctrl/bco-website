Privacy Policy for B.CO

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

B.CO ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it is stored, and what choices you have when using B.CO.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use B.CO, we may process account information such as your email address, account identifier, display name, profile information, profile photo, and account settings or preferences.

When you use communication features, we may process content you choose to provide, including chat messages, group messages, Stories, Story Replies, images or other media you upload, and GIFs or other shared content.

We may also receive technical information needed to operate and secure the application, such as device information, operating system information, application version, technical identifiers, and error or diagnostic information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your B.CO account.
  • To provide chat, groups, Stories, Story Replies, calls, and notifications.
  • To store and deliver content that you choose to upload or share.
  • To maintain, improve, and troubleshoot the application.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, security issues, or violations of our terms.
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. Firebase and Third-Party Services

B.CO may use Google Firebase services to provide authentication, database storage, cloud storage, notifications, and related backend functionality. These services may include Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Storage, and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

For more information, see Google Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate B.CO, when necessary to provide the service, protect security, prevent fraud or abuse, or comply with legal obligations. Content and profile information may be visible to other B.CO users when you intentionally use features designed for sharing.

5. User Content

B.CO allows users to create and share content. You should only upload or share content that you have the right to use and that you are comfortable sharing with the intended recipients.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through B.CO. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide requested services, maintain accounts, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the security of the application. When an account deletion request is processed, we initiate deletion of eligible account data, subject to legal, security, and technical requirements.

8. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your B.CO account from inside the application. You can also submit a deletion request through our public Account Deletion page.

9. Children's Privacy

B.CO is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about processing. Contact us to exercise an applicable request.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last Updated" date when changes are made.
